## Narrow test-precondition issue, not a replacement policy

Task§7 includes an “omitted one of two same-ID deletions” negative. This is testable if the omitted original remains in an unchanged signed declared set/digest, or is independently supplied as a known local original fact/deny restriction. It is **not detectable** if an approved pinned administrator omits an otherwise unknown original and coherently reattests every record/set/digest while local observations contain no evidence of it. Decision§1/§4 expressly preserves administrator-attested completeness and says surviving signatures cannot prove absence of omitted events. Thus a test expecting rejection of the latter input without additional evidence would be impossible and would contradict that already explicit trust boundary. The brief need not be rewritten to implement the specified checks: make the negative's independent evidence source explicit, and separately preserve the coherent-omission limit. This is an oracle/precondition clarification, not an authorization to infer missing facts or invent unsigned admission chronology.

No other concrete impossible or contradictory task clause was identified in the read scope. In particular: refusing member_removed facts while supporting commit-only removals is coherent; permitting false final-digest inputs to yield only the limited candidate is deliberate; historical tombstone basis is checkpoint-only and does not require changing the live M1 loader; absent native scheduling yields NOT RUN/incomplete validation rather than permission to use the active runner.
